## Limitations

- One market, one ~6.7-month window, a handful of trades: far too small a sample to estimate an edge. The result mostly reflects this window's gold regime (see buy-and-hold); a trend rule earns when gold trends and loses in chop, so a different window can give a very different number.
- Prices are Binance PAXGUSDT. The Wallex PAXGUSDT market is thin, wide and trades at a varying discount to Binance (table above), so fills there would differ; XAUTUSDT on Wallex is the realistic venue.
- The cost model is the repo's assumption set (0.2 % taker fee unverified for our tier, 7.5 bps half-spread, a volume impact proxy). No Wallex depth history exists for gold beyond the snapshots above.
- 1h bars: nothing is known inside a bar; stops fill at the stop (or the open on a gap), and the stop is assumed hit first when one bar touches both. The shadow replay checks stops on 5m prices instead.
- The repo's 25 % max position caps what any strategy can earn here: a fully invested trade gains a quarter of gold's move. S3 shows the same signals without the caps.
