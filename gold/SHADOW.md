## Why shadow and backtest differ

- **Entry.** The engine fills at the next 1h bar's open plus half-spread and impact. The bot evaluates the closed bar at the first tick after it closes, sends a LIMIT at ask + 10 bps through the OMS, and ShadowBroker fills it about 2 s later at the synthetic ask (the last quote close + 7.5 bps).
- **Stops.** The engine assumes a touched stop fills exactly at the stop (or at the open after a gap) using the 1h low. The bot only sees quote closes (5m / 15m): it sells at the bid after a close below the stop (usually below the stop level), or, if only a wick inside one quote candle touched it, on the next 1h close via its `stop_touched_on_bar` check. That is the main source of exit-price differences.
- **Fees.** ShadowBroker charges the buy fee in the base asset (PAXG) and the sell fee in USDT; the engine charges both in USDT. Same value, slightly different quantities (the bot sells what it actually holds).
- **Sizing and rounding.** Both size with the repo's `size_position` (3 % risk, 25 % position cap), the engine at open x (1 + half-spread), the bot at its limit price and current equity; stops are put on the price tick by the engine only.
- **Risk-engine rejections (a finding).** The bot sizes with `size_position` (25 % of equity at the limit price) and the OMS then runs the risk engine, which adds the value of any PAXG still held to the new order. ShadowBroker charges the buy fee in PAXG, so after the first round trip a dust remainder (below one amount step, unsellable) stays in the account; a new entry sized to exactly the 25 % cap then exceeds it by that dust and is refused (`above_max_position_pct`). The backtest engine sizes and checks with one formula and never sees this. The refused entries are listed under each window above; the entry happens only on a later signal whose rounding leaves room under the cap. This is a sizing-vs-risk mismatch in the repo's bot path that only shows when the position cap binds; it is reported, not patched here.
- **Cooldown.** Both wait 24 bars after an exit; the bot's tracker also refuses a re-entry on the same breakout level after a losing stop-out.
- **Book.** The replay book is synthetic and deep (no impact); the real Wallex PAXGUSDT book is far thinner and wider (REPORT.md, 'Wallex reality check'). `gold-shadow --live` runs the same stack on the real Wallex book.
